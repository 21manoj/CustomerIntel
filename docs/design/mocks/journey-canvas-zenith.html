Design notes

Rendered from the new build's journey v3 for this account — nothing here is hand-typed. Three bands share one time axis: the state header (now), the story (past) and the forecast (next), with the now line as a scrubber so the forecast can be audited against what was known earlier. Realized ARR movement is drawn solid; narrative outcomes hollow. Signals are placed by role and colored by polarity with a shape as the second channel; where the source sentiment contradicts the role, the mark is flagged rather than trusted. Forward statements carry their basis and, on this tenant, say plainly that no loss event exists to calibrate against.
