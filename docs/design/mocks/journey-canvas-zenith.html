Design notes

Rendered from the new build's journey v3 for this account — nothing here is hand-typed. Three bands share one time axis: the state header (now), the story (past) and the forecast (next), with the now line as a scrubber so the forecast can be audited against what was known earlier. Realized ARR movement is drawn solid; narrative outcomes hollow. Signals sit by role and are colored by polarity with a shape as the second channel; where the source sentiment contradicts the role, the mark is flagged rather than trusted. Every forward mark on the canvas has a row in the forecast ledger stating its basis; on this tenant the likelihood fan is a prior, and says so.
