What changed from the health-first canvas

The spine is the evidence, not the score. Phases are read from the pressure of negative-role evidence in the window, so deterioration is dated from the first negative signal rather than from the month the KPI slope turned. People are nodes with typed edges — about (customer-side, from the signal's own stakeholder), owns (our side, from the action) — and a person with no evidence is shown as exactly that, never as engaged. Chains are drawn at their evidence tier: the source's asserted links solid, precedence-within-window dotted and labelled inferred, and a suspicious pattern (every outcome asserting the same first signal) flagged rather than trusted. The KPI line is a toggle. Coverage — sources, last touch, unresolved people, months without evidence — sits in the header because on a signals-first platform, silence must be distinguished from absence of logging before it can mean anything.
